Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Elite Cleaning ("we", "us", or "our") provides premium dry cleaning and laundry services through the Elite Cleaning mobile application (the "App") and related website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and/or mobile phone number when you register or log in to the App.
  • Service information: pickup and delivery addresses, order history, payment history, and order details you submit.
  • Device information: device push token and notification identifier used to deliver order status updates and service notifications.
  • Photo information: images and photos you choose to upload through the App (e.g., profile photo or order-related photos).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and provide our laundry and dry cleaning services.
  • To process and track your orders, schedule pickups and deliveries, and send order status notifications.
  • To process payments through our payment processors.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To send important service-related communications and notifications.
  • To improve and personalize your experience with the App.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the App and deliver services (e.g., payment processing via Stripe, cloud notifications via Firebase).
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Payments

Online payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers. Card information is handled directly by Stripe in accordance with their privacy and security practices.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. All communication with our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account at any time through the App. After such request, your account and associated personal data will be deleted automatically within a maximum of 3 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date above and, where appropriate, through the App.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at support@elitecleanps.com.
